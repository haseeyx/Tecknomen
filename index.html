About Teknomen

We build practical engineering for the floors of Pakistan’s spinning mills, where a stopped frame costs money every minute.

Teknomen specializes in industrial automation, VFDs, customized control systems, spindle monitoring, industrial power backup, and PCB design and fabrication, along with custom solutions for specific production needs.

FAQ

Questions mills ask us.

What industries do you serve?

Mainly textile and spinning mills, plus manufacturing plants, energy and power facilities, packaging lines, pharmaceutical producers and food and beverage operations. Each solution is adapted to the industry’s own requirements.

Do you install and commission on site?

Yes. Every project includes on-site installation, calibration, operator training and a documented handover. Our engineers stay involved through the first production cycle.

Can you add automation to machines we already have?

Yes. Retrofit is one of our core strengths. SpinGuard Pro, for example, adds automation to existing ring spinning frames without new hardware or changes to the base machine.

What after-sales support do you provide?

Preventive maintenance schedules, spare parts, remote diagnostics, firmware upgrades and on-site response. Support agreements are set to how critical the line is to your production.

How long does a typical project take?

Small retrofits and single-panel systems usually take 2 to 4 weeks. Full production-line automation takes 6 to 12 weeks from design sign-off, depending on scope. You get a documented timeline before work starts.
