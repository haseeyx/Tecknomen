Overview

TEKNOMEN provides professional electronics repair and maintenance services for industrial and commercial equipment. Our services focus on troubleshooting faulty electronic systems, identifying board-level failures and restoring equipment to reliable operating condition.

We specialize in PCB and industrial electronics repair, including VFD drives and other control and power electronic systems.

Each repair begins with a technical assessment to identify the probable fault and determine the appropriate repair approach before restoration and testing.

What we do

Minimize downtime. Restore your equipment.
